# Purpose-bound fixed card acquisition, 2026-10-23

User contract: paid acquisition is exceptional and needs an explicit purpose: a spare income card, one critical move/data corner for a concrete reachable target, a demonstrably strong known card, or publicity with no remaining technology use. Picking which card is deterministic plan execution, like movement, not a search choice. Unchanged known offer plus unchanged purpose reuses the card decision, regardless of payment ordering or wallet state.

Own card offers remain native rule Decisions and replay inputs. Search compacts fixed acquisition/payment inputs into plan edges and charges each native input and internal state against the existing global 256 input / 257 node / 256 frontier / 600ms diagnostic budget. Every internal step checks time and remaining global capacity; no depth/leaf cap is treated as pruning. No full games until single-decision validation passes.

State equivalence: full rule identity remains unchanged. A derived committed acquisition purpose, selected card and payment set are included separately in control identity when reconstructed from the original native Session base. Ranking cache keys use semantic card offer facts, purpose/required role and round; never hidden draw order, raw action envelope versions, or payment path. Cache stores the card identity, then binds the current exact legal descriptor. Reset/restore creates a new instance/cache, close retains only bounded ranking entries.

Reachability: demands must name their target and missing role; movement/data purposes require exactly one missing point and preserve endpoint funds. No blind acquisition is generated for a specifically required movement/data role. Income requires an upcoming funded fourth computer slot and a genuine lack of spare cards. Unused publicity requires no takeable technology and no known retained publicity scoring need. Strong-card admission uses known unconditional net payoff and is explicitly a policy, not equivalence. Unresolved/unsupported purpose is reported, never guessed from hidden cards.

Scope: ordinary public-card acquisition from quick trades and free card/science pick effects. Scan card/nebula selection, income insertion and setup selection are different goals, not conflated with acquisition. Existing owner/information stopping semantics remain; this change does not claim turn-end coverage or full-game acceptance.

Acceptance: no-goal trade exclusions; income/move/data/strong-card/unused-publicity positive and negative cases; exactly one fixed pick; cache reuse across wallet/payment changes; invalidation when offer/purpose changes; hidden-world independence; every macro native input counted and replayed separately; budget interruption yields no submit action/token; warm/cold restore parity; root/RNG unchanged. Existing eight fixed points are measured after frozen-source tests, without relabelling their failures.
