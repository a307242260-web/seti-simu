# Goal-bound greedy resource preparation

User contract: no independent resource exchange or discard-corner search. A concrete action/reward goal owns its resource demand. Greedy preparation stops when funded; only demonstrably unresolved, non-equivalent effect/payment choices may enter target-bounded fallback search with a recorded reason.

Goals: card play (protect its card), scan, launch, named research tile/blue slot, named ordinary probe endpoint, computer income/analyze, and blue data bonuses. Native costs and structural options are queried read-only. Ordinary directly funded actions remain; loose exchange/corner/computer-placement inputs are removed and represented by goal-plan alternatives. Card acquisition retains its existing explicit-purpose policy.

Greedy selection: meet the exact deficit, protect the target card and needed movement/data roles, use cheapest lost resource value, then fewest native inputs, then stable identity. Every admitted preparation strictly reduces unmet demand or a fixed placement obligation. Optimistic resource capacity rejects unreachable goals. Unknown/identity-sensitive triggered decisions remain bound to the goal; they never reopen the unqualified preparation tree.

State equivalence: complete native state/queue identity remains unchanged. A fallback's goal and fixed-operation context are additional search-control identity. Pure greedy intermediates are execution states, not action-selection graph nodes. Every actual native input and internal state is charged to the same global input/node/frontier/time budget; no local depth or leaf cap. Single-decision measurement precedes games.

Recovery: no hidden mutable goal store. After a completed atomic preparation, replan deterministically from the physical state; do not issue internal prefix tokens that assume an unpersisted goal. During a native preparation Session, recover candidate beneficiary goals from its saved base and committed source. Each real machine run still submits one input/replay event. Warm and restored-cold behavior must match.

Acceptance: no-demand exclusion; minimal funding and protected cards; no over-preparation; fixed payments and data/income; structural unreachable goals; explicit bounded fallback and no unqualified actions; interrupted budget yields no submit/token; native root/RNG immutability; per-click recovery; fixed corpus and native input replay. Advanced card-effect resource budgets and company-specific demands must be explicitly accounted for or listed as unsupported, never silently approximated as native-exhaustive coverage.
